# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Standard returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided
they are unused and in original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the item.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 7 days of
delivery with photos of the damage. We will send a prepaid return label and
issue a full refund or replacement — no restocking fee applies.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, downloadable software, and personalized/custom-made products
cannot be returned unless defective.

## International orders
International orders can be returned within 45 days. The customer is
responsible for return shipping costs unless the item was defective.
