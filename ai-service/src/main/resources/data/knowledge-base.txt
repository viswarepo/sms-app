========================================================================
ACME CORP INTERNAL OPERATION MANUAL & POLICY GUIDE (VERSION 2026.1)
========================================================================

1. OFFICE LOGISTICS & ACCESS
The Acme Corp main headquarters is located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Sector 7G.
The office building is open to employees Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
Weekend access requires prior authorization from the Facilities Team via the internal Slack channel #hq-access at least 24 hours in advance.

2. REMOTE WORK & REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
All full-time employees are eligible for a monthly remote work stipend of INR 4,500 to cover internet utilities and home office consumables.
Expensing hardware items (like keyboards, monitors, or ergonomic chairs) must go through the IT Procurement Portal and requires approval from your direct department head.

3. WIFI NETWORK CREDENTIALS
- Primary Employee Network SSID: Acme_Secure_Corp
- Authentication Protocol: WPA3 Enterprise (Log in using your corporate email and SSO password)
- Guest Network SSID: Acme_Guest_WiFi (Password: RoadRunner2026!)
Note: Do not connect personal streaming devices or gaming consoles to the primary network.

4. HEALTH & WELLNESS BENEFITS
Acme Corp partners with OmniHealth Insurance.
The standard health package covers 100% of preventive care, routine checkups, and prescription drugs up to a limit of INR 500,000 per family unit per year.
Mental health support is provided through the "MindSpace" app, which offers 10 free therapy sessions per calendar year for all active employees.

5. HOLIDAY SCHEDULE & FLOATING LEAVE
The company observes 12 standard public holidays.
Additionally, employees receive 3 "Personal Innovation Days" per year.
These are floating leave days meant for working on side projects, learning new engineering skills, or prototyping fresh product ideas.
Unused Innovation Days do not roll over to the next calendar year.

6. EXPENSE REPORTS & MILEAGE
When traveling for client deployment, employees can claim a travel reimbursement rate of INR 45 per kilometer driven in a personal vehicle.
All expense claims exceeding INR 2,000 require a scanned copy of the original physical receipt or an official e-invoice.
Submissions must be finalized in the ExpenseBot software by the 25th day of each month.
